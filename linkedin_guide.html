01 Headline Most visible field · shows in every search result

This is the single most important field on LinkedIn. It shows under your name in every search, feed post, and message. Recruiters search by keywords — if your headline doesn't have the words, you won't appear.

→ Copy this exactly
AI Automation Builder · Make.com & n8n · Software Engineer · Content & Media Strategy MSc · Netherlands

Why this works: Leads with what you build (not your title), names the exact tools recruiters search for (Make.com, n8n), establishes the rare dual background, and includes Netherlands for local search visibility. 96 characters — well within the limit.
